## 👋 Hey, I'm Sanjid

EEE undergrad at KUET, graduating October 2027. I work on making speech recognition small enough to run on the devices people actually have — no cloud, no GPU required.

My current obsession: **Bangla ASR**. 230 million speakers, chronically underserved by mainstream systems. I'm fixing that one paper at a time.

- 🔬 Founding member of **KMinds**, KUET's student AI research community
- ⚡ Founder of **Team Frozen Voltage** — AI and project building at KUET

- 🗺️ Google Maps **Level 7 Local Guide** (1,000+ contributions in Khulna)
- 🏆 Kaggle **Datasets Expert**

## 📄 Selected Papers

🏅 **[Tokenizer Transplantation: Mitigating Autoregressive Collapse in Edge-Efficient Bengali ASR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09598)**  
`ICML 2026 Workshop` `Muslims in ML` `2nd prize KUET University Day`  
Replacing an English byte-level tokenizer with BanglaBERT's WordPiece cuts decoding length by 85.8% and eliminates autoregressive collapse.

🏆 **[Beyond Recognition Accuracy: Efficient CPU Streaming ASR for Dysarthric Speech](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2026/181064)**  
`NeurIPS 2026 Competition Track` `Talk · non-archival`  
Led team KMinds at the Speech Accessibility Project Challenge 2 — real-time dysarthric ASR on a CPU.

📦 **[Lipi-Ghor: A Large-Scale, Diarized, Multi-Domain Bengali Speech Corpus](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36184)**  
`EMNLP 2026` `SALMA Workshop pp. 190–201`  
882 hours of speaker-labelled Bengali speech + a scalable diarization pipeline. [Dataset →](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Sanjidh090/Lipi-Ghor-bn-882-SSTT)

📝 **[Make It Hard to Hear, Easy to Learn: Long-Form Bengali ASR via Extreme Augmentation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.23070)**  
`arXiv preprint` `DL Sprint 4.0 podium`  
Training on heavily degraded audio beats adding more data. Pipeline runs at ~0.019 RTF.
